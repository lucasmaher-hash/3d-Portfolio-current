Overview / Concept

0
Recipe Steps 1

Click the panel to step through the recipe instructions one by one. Each step lights up a tile on the progress strip as you work through the dish.

Ingredients 2

Tap + or − to adjust the portion count and all ingredient quantities scale live. The hourglass tracks how full the cooking vessel is.

Cooking Timer 3

A 3D stopwatch ring counts down as you cook. Hit play to start, pause to hold — the ring fills to signal when the dish is ready.

Process

5 Steps  ·  Concept → Build
Blender scene
Concept 1

Defining the scope: a 3D kitchen environment where each recipe step is spatially mapped to real objects in the scene.

Sketch / wireframe
Research 2

Studying spatial UI patterns and existing AR cooking apps to identify where 3D adds genuine value over flat interfaces.

3D environment
Modelling 3

Kitchen props and ingredient objects modelled in Blender, optimised for real-time rendering and exported as .glb.

Interaction prototype
Interaction 4

Click-to-inspect objects, step-by-step progression, and ambient cooking sounds wired up in vanilla JS.

Final build
Polish 5

Lighting refinement, transition animations, and responsive fallback for mobile — matching the portfolio's design language throughout.
