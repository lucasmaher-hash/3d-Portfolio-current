Unify

At a Glance

Unify started from a problem in my own campus life: my friends study different subjects, so even though we're all on the same campus, we're rarely in the same courses. Meeting up between classes meant memorising everyone's timetable or texting around every break. We began by swapping screenshots of our schedules — Unify turns that into one place where you see your own timetable alongside your friends'. Open it during a break and you can tell at a glance who's on campus right now and who you can meet up with.

Choosing the right colorpalette

We chose three soft, pastel-leaning colours: pink and beige as the primaries, with a dark grey as the accent. The goal was a look that feels young, student-y and cheerful — so we picked a bold, slightly unusual pink you don't often see in apps.

Character based design

Characters are the core of Unify's visual identity — the whole app is built around them, giving it a consistent, friendly personality from screen to screen.

Their main role is personalisation: you choose a character as your profile icon, so your friends can spot and recognise you at a glance across the app.

The characters also live inside the interface itself — turning up in details like the bar on the map view or the toggle between your timetable and social activities.

Final Product

2

Your Day at a Glance

The home screen is your quick daily overview. At a glance it shows who you can meet up with right now or in your next break — which friends are free, and exactly when. Below that sit the day's social activities, and a map of how many friends are around you at this moment. Everything important lives on one scrollable page; when something catches your eye, the dedicated pages let you dig into the detail.

Your Timetable, and Everyone's

See your own timetable alongside your friends', and who shares each course. Your schedule splits in two: pink for regular courses, beige for the social activities you add from the Socials page. Below it sit your friends' timetables — and thanks to location tracking, the app knows when you're on campus: the room you're in glows green and pushes you up the list, while a grey room means you're away.

Beyond the Group Chat

Every uni has that one group chat where events and social plans get buried under hundreds of messages. Socials replaces it — activities get posted here instead, so nothing slips through. Scroll through the week, and when something appeals to you, add it straight to your timetable in a tap.

Find Your Friends Indoors

Apps like Find My are great for locating friends — until everyone's inside the same building and the map turns useless. Unify syncs room-level location with the university's floor plans, so you can see exactly who's around you on the map, or search for someone specific from the menu.

Find the Right Room

The same idea, now for your courses. Universities are huge — hundreds of rooms across sprawling buildings — and working out where each class is can be overwhelming, especially when you're new. Unify syncs with your timetable to plot every course room on the map; tap one to see the full details of that class.

Profile & Friends

The menu is where you set yourself up. Customise your profile, and connect your courses in one step — everything syncs automatically from your matriculation number. It's also home to your friends list, where you add or remove people whenever you need.
