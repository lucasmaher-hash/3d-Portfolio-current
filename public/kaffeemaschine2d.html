Final result

2

Coffee machines have some of the most forgettable interfaces in the world. I wanted to take that exact mundanity and push it somewhere visually specific — a retro-futuristic 2000s aesthetic that feels like it belongs on a device pulled from a 2003 sci-fi film. The interface is fully interactive: six physical side buttons map live to whatever's on screen, wrong presses shake the display and fire a synthesized buzz, and the CRT scanlines and Digital-7 Mono readout lock in the era. Designed in Figma, then rebuilt pixel-perfect in HTML — as much a coding exercise as a design one.
